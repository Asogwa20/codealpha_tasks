Quick Help

  • Upload: Use the Upload button or drag and drop images into My Gallery or Dashboard.
  • Organize: Add titles, descriptions, categories, and tags from an image card's Edit details action. Create albums and add images to multiple albums.
  • Find images: Use the top search bar for your gallery, and filters/sort in My Gallery. Explore Images searches Wikimedia Commons.
  • Favourites: Click the heart icon on any image to mark or unmark it.
  • Edit: Open an image and choose Edit to crop, rotate, flip, resize, adjust brightness/contrast, or apply filters. Edited results are saved as copies.
  • Lightbox: Click an image to preview full screen. Use ←/→ to navigate and Esc to close.
  • Trash: Deleted gallery images move to Trash where you can restore or permanently delete them.
  • Storage: Images are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Clearing browser data may remove them.
  • Licensing: When importing from Wikimedia Commons, review the image licence and provide attribution as required by the licence.
